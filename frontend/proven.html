What Proven ships

Three protocols. One trust source.

01
DojangScore
Reads four Dojang attestations — verified address, verified balance, account age, verified code — and returns one composite trust score, 0–1000, in four tiers. The primitive every other protocol on GIWA can gate on.
→ the credit bureau of the chain
02
WonVault
An ERC-4626 KRW yield vault where your trust tier sets your entry fee, not your yield — 2.00% unverified down to 0.50% at Platinum. Hardened against the ERC-4626 inflation attack we caught live and patched.
→ verifying literally keeps more of your money earning
03
GIWASwap
The first DEX on GIWA — a constant-product AMM where verified wallets pay a lower swap fee (0.30% → 0.10%). A bot that stays anonymous pays triple; the cheapest Sybil-resistance marketing a chain will ever run.
→ trust, finally, has a price
Trust shouldn't be a claim. It should be a receipt you can read on-chain — and a fee you pay for ignoring it.the Proven principle
