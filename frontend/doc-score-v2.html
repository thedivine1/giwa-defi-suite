How the live ladder works: a wallet that merely holds vault shares reads Silver from the trustless balanceOf lookup — no Upbit, no reporter. Submit a proof (you vouch for yourself) → ceiling opens to Gold. A reporter (the indexer, or the deployer via the kick) adds behavioral activity. Platinum requires the regulated attester — on testnet, the owner's stand-in override. Self-attestation cannot reach Platinum: that cap is the whole point.
Model ↔ contract parity: category caps (attest 1000 · behavioral 250 · consented 250 · state 100) and ceilings (behavioral 499 · consented 799 · attester 1000) are identical in this simulator and in DojangScoreV2.sol. The simulator shows per-signal detail; on-chain stores capped category aggregates + provenance classes (cheaper, audit-friendly). The two agree on the final number.
