What it does · features

Reads Dojang, outputs trust

  • Verified Address — Upbit KYC confirmation via isVerified() (400 pts).
  • Verified Balance — exchange-held funds attested on-chain (300 pts).
  • Account age — attestation older than 90 days, read from EAS (200 pts).
  • Verified Code — 2FA / authentication attestation (100 pts).
  • Graceful reads — every attestation call is try/catch wrapped, so a missing or revoked proof degrades the score instead of reverting the call.
  • tierOf() / tierName() — Unverified / Silver / Gold / Platinum at 200 / 500 / 800.
  • Tunable weights + owner-settable manual scores for bootstrapping and demo.
Value to the protocol

Chapter two · the yield engine

WonVault

An ERC-4626 KRW yield vault where your trust tier sets your entry fee, not your yield. The most intellectually dense contract in the suite — and the one we hardened against a real exploit we caught live.

How it earns APY · the method

Share-price accrual, not a printed number

  • The vault is a standard ERC-4626: it holds the asset (testnet TKRW; mainnet the Dunamu/Naver KRW stablecoin) and issues shares.
  • Yield shows up as the share price rising above 1.0 — because totalAssets() grows while the share count stays flat.
  • On testnet the source is simulated (a "strategy tick" injects profit) so the engine is visible. On mainnet the source is real and external: lending interest · AMM trading fees · staking/consensus rewards · KRW sovereign / treasury coupons (RWA).
  • Deposits are price-neutral. A latecomer deposits at the current price and gets a fair share count — they do not fund earlier depositors. Only accrued external yield moves the price.
The intelligence behind it

Trust sets the entry fee → a flywheel

  • On every deposit, feeTierOf() reads DojangScore and skims a tiered entry fee to treasury: Standard 2.00% · Silver 1.50% · Gold 1.00% · Platinum 0.50%.
  • The net amount is then handed to OpenZeppelin's own audited deposit path — so the share math is library code, not hand-rolled.
  • Result: verifying through Dojang literally keeps more of your money earning from day one. That is a direct economic incentive to complete verification — which drives Dojang adoption — which is GIWA's own KPI.
  • previewDepositAfterFee() shows the user the exact fee before they sign.
